Sum up 20 November's world news in one image: a dense 1024×1024 newspaper
front page — **30 stories, 6 regions**, Friday 20 November 2026.

## What is on the page

- **Masthead:** THE WORLD TODAY — FRIDAY 20 NOVEMBER 2026.
- **Lead story:** the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen
  ("Three leaders, one table"), with the three countries' name chips, as APEC
  gathers in China.
- **Landfall box:** Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crosses the Andhra
  Pradesh–Odisha coast near Gopalpur at noon on 20 November — winds 90–100
  km/h, gusting to 110; 120,000+ evacuated; 65+ trains cancelled; schools
  shut in 13 districts.
- **Story grid:** 12 headed stories with 2–3 sentence explainers — the
  Crimean Bridge hit again and embassy damage in Kyiv; the ICJ genocide
  verdict on Myanmar; Seoul's martial-law retrial; Fiji's drought disaster;
  the US–Venezuela military pact; North Korea's enrichment growth; Modi
  meets the Prince and Princess of Wales; Nicholls' 113* in Wellington;
  markets rally and Anthropic's reported $1.5tn valuation; the Dhaka arson;
  Iran's 2,500+ missiles; COP31 in Türkiye.
- **Stat strip:** 3 leaders at one table · 120,000+ evacuated · 2,500+
  missiles still held by Iran · 10,000 killed, Rohingya 2017 · $1.5tn
  Anthropic valuation · 30 stories, 6 regions.
- **ALSO TODAY:** 16 more one-line headlines, from World Children's Day to
  the EPPO's €213.7m rail-fraud charge.
- **Winner of a blind panel:** three fresh-context judges, all three pairs,
  both orders — v1 beat the poster variant 2–0 (margin 2, then margin 3
  after fixes) and the atlas variant 3–0. The panel's fixes (drop the
  abstract map strip for a stat strip, enlarge story type, put the cyclone
  wind figure on its own line) are in this image.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output
directory. Two runs write byte-identical output. `entry.png` is 1024×1024
and under 1 MiB.

## Provenance

All 30 stories were verified against live reporting on 20 November 2026
(Reuters, AP, Le Monde, Seoul Economic Daily, IMD bulletins, school-assembly
headline roundups dated 20 Nov). Nothing is claimed for events still ahead;
forecasts are labelled as forecasts (Akvan landfall "at noon", rain "scarce
until April"). Background dates (Rohingya 2017, US–Venezuela ties "severed
since 2005") are framed as background, never as 20 November news.
